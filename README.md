# 🍲 Hawker Rush: The Ultimate Stall Defense! 🍲

Wah lau! The hawker center is under attack! Not by health inspectors, but by waves of hungry customers rushing for their favorite food. Can you hold the fort (and the floor) or will you be overwhelmed by the lunchtime crowd?

**Hawker Rush** is a fast-paced tower defense game where you become the ultimate Hawker Boss. Deploy your best stalls, upgrade your recipe, and earn those prestigious Kitchelin Stars!

---

## 🏃‍♂️ The Hungry Crowd (Enemies)

They’re coming for your food, and they’re not patient!
- **Salaryman:** Quick and hungry. If you don't feed them fast, they'll bolt past you!
- **Tourist:** They like to stop and take photos. Use this chance to blast them with flavor!
- **Auntie:** The real boss. Very tanky, very determined. Don't mess with her.
- **Tiger Mom:** Supporting her family with a 90% armor buff. Sibei troublesome!
- **Delivery Rider:** The Boss of bosses. Fast on dry land, but watch them slip on Teh Tarik puddles!

---

## 🏗️ Your Hawker Arsenal (Stalls)

From beverages to heavy meals, you've got everything:
- **Chicken Rice:** The GOAT. Fast, reliable single-target damage.
- **Teh Tarik:** Pull some tea and slow them down with delicious puddles!
- **Satay:** AOE damage that hits like a spice-bomb.
- **Ice Kachang:** Freeze them in their tracks. Literally.
- **Durian:** High damage, but slow fire rate. The King of Fruits doesn't rush.
- **Tray Return Uncle:** He’ll grab them and hold them back. "Return your tray, please!"
- **Bak Kut Teh:** Boosts the damage and effects of all adjacent stalls. Teamwork makes the dream work!
- **ATM:** Because no money, no talk. Generates passive income.

---

## ✨ Become a Legend (Stall Names)

Upgrade your stalls enough and they’ll earn their reputation! Hit Level 10 in a category and watch your humble "Chicken Rice" transform into **"Colossal Chicken Rice the Annihilator"**.

The more you upgrade, the more *garang* your stalls become. Don't play play!

---

## ⭐ The Kitchelin Stars

Every 10 waves, if you're still standing, you'll earn a **Kitchelin Star**. These aren't just for show! Use them to:
- **Boost Your Budget:** 100% bonus gold for the next wave. Huat ah!
- **Renovate for Free:** Skip the cost and the wait for those specific upgrades.
- **Restore Health:** Fix up your health when things get a bit too "hot".
- **Renovate the Floor:** Spend a star to remove a Pillar and make more space for your stalls.

---

## 🛠️ How to Play

1.  **Place Stalls:** Use your starting cash to build your defense.
2.  **Upgrade:** Tap on a stall to make it stronger. Upgrades are random, unless you pay extra for a specific one!
3.  **Targeting:** Change targeting (First, Closest, Strongest, Weakest) to optimize your strategy.
4.  **Survive:** Don't let the customers reach the Goal Table!
